TETRIS-MOBILE CAR SPRITES

Free Racing Car #1 (64x64) by Pay 'n' Sprite
https://patricio449.itch.io/free-racing-car-1-64x64
Source archive: mstg_gtd.zip
Retrieved 28 October 2026.

Included files are unmodified source sprites with opaque windows:
car-rear.png  <- gtd_op_frame_0.png
car-front.png <- gtd_op_front_view.png
car-left.png  <- gtd_op_turn_left.png
car-right.png <- gtd_op_turn_right.png

The creator permits use in commercial and non-commercial projects and permits
colour changes and variants. Redistribution or resale as an asset on other
marketplaces, NFT projects, and generative AI training are prohibited. The
creator's page above contains the complete current terms. These sprites are
included as part of the cinematic, not as a standalone asset pack.

The Tetris trim, scanner, night tint and light animations are separate rendering
layers. A simple original pixel car is retained as an image-load fallback.

CHESS SCENERY

Sky, clouds, columns, ivy, ruins, temple, dolphin and beach props are reused
from the existing chess-cinematic.js. The Deep-Fold MIT notice is preserved
in tetris-cinematic-scenery.js alongside the extracted shader.
